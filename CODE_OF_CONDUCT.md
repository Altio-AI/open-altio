# Code of conduct

Open Altio is for anyone who wants to look at drug safety data, so everyone who takes part should be able to do so without harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Take no part in harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexual language or attention, or publishing someone's private details.

This applies in this repository's issues and pull requests, and wherever someone speaks for the project.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainers privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Altio-AI/open-altio/security/advisories/new), and say it is about conduct. Only the maintainers see it. We aim to reply within a week and keep the name of whoever reported it private.

Maintainers may edit or remove what breaks this code, and may warn or ban whoever keeps breaking it.
